percent to goal

Gross AUM YTD divided by the annual Gross AUM goal, expressed as a percentage.

projected percent to goal

Projected year-end production divided by the annual Gross AUM goal, expressed as a percentage.

goal outlook

On Track is projected at 100% or more of goal; Close (the dashboard's at-risk category) is 85% through 99.9%; Off Track is below 85%.

appointments

Completed advisor activities in the selected reporting period. Intro includes intro or discovery activity; Review includes completed review activity; weekly average is completed appointments divided by elapsed reporting weeks.

networking

Networking events recorded for the advisor in the selected reporting period. The annual scorecard target is derived from the advisor's weekly SMART goal.

household roles

Primary is the originating advisor; Secondary is credited for eligible new money after assignment; Servicing is responsible for the ongoing relationship and reviews.

average household aum

Book AUM divided by household count for the applicable advisor role. The dashboard target is $500,000.

missing values

A numeric zero means measured activity or value of zero. null means the value is unavailable from the current source. Fields are never omitted merely because they are zero or unavailable.
